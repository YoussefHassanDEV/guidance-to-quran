Billing FAQ

Questions about fees

How do I pay?
We accept PayPal, debit/credit cards, bank transfer and Wise. You receive an invoice by email at the start of each month; there are no automatic charges without your consent.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes. If you are unhappy within the first 7 days of a paid month, we refund the month in full. After that, unused classes are carried forward or refunded pro-rata.
Can I change my plan or class time later?
Any time. Message your coordinator on WhatsApp and the change applies from the next class.
Are there any hidden fees?
None. No registration fee, no material fee, no cancellation fee. The price you see is the price you pay.
What if none of the plans fit my schedule?
Ask for a custom programme. Tell us how many classes you want, your availability and your goals, and we will quote a price built around you.
