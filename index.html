What age can my child start?
From age 4 with Noorani Qaida and Daily Duas. Lessons for little ones are 30 minutes with songs, stories and lots of praise.
What do we need at home?
A laptop, tablet or phone with internet, and Zoom or Skype. We send all materials digitally, so there is nothing to buy.
Can I sit in on the class?
Always. Parents are welcome in every lesson and can request recordings.
Can we choose a female teacher?
Yes. Tell us your preference on the free-trial form and we will match you with a qualified Hafizah or Alimah.
What happens after the free trial?
Nothing automatic. If you loved it, pick a plan and keep the same teacher. If not, no charge and no questions.
How do I pay, and can I cancel?
Monthly invoice by PayPal, card or bank transfer. Cancel any time; unused classes are refunded pro-rata.
